How the Indirect Lever Models work

Indirect levers — like tuition, work-experience averages, or applicant demographics — aren't ranked by US News directly. Instead, each of the eight indicator-level models translates these levers into a predicted indicator value. The Direct Score Model then turns the predicted indicators into a rank.

Each card below = one of the 8 indicator-level models. The confidence badge tells you how trustworthy that model is — based on how well it predicts schools it was not trained on (cross-validation) and on a forward-in-time test (train on 2024, test on 2025). Every lever's % contribution to that model's prediction is listed directly beneath each card.

High — trust the direction and rough magnitude Medium — direction is reliable, magnitude is rough Low — noisy signal; treat as suggestive only

Reading the card metrics: Cross-val fit is how much of the indicator's variation the model can explain on schools it wasn't trained on (1.00 = perfect, near 0 = no better than the cohort average). Next-year fit repeats the same test forward in time — train on 2024 data, predict 2025. Avg error is the typical prediction miss in the indicator's own units (percentage points for rates, dollars for salary, etc.). Trained on is the number of school-year observations the model learned from.

How the Direct Score Model works

This is the engine that converts the 9 ranking indicators on the Direct Predictor tab into a predicted overall score. It's a machine-learning regression trained on two years of US News data (10,000 training rounds, calibrated so the #1 school = score 100).

Use this tab to see which indicators move the rank the most, how reliably the model predicts a school's score, and how confident we are in each individual weight.

How well does the model match US News?

Four ways of measuring accuracy on the same 243 school-year observations.

MAE & RMSE — average score error (lower is better; published scores run 0–100). R² — share of score variation the model explains (1.00 = perfect). Spearman ρ — agreement on rank order (1.00 = identical ranking).

How much does each indicator move the score?

sorted by impact size

Each row shows one indicator's impact weight (how many points a one-standard-deviation improvement adds to the predicted score) and an uncertainty range from re-training the model on 10,000 random data slices. A row is significant when the range stays on one side of zero — meaning we're confident the direction is right.
